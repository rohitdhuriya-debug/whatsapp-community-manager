How to get a token and connect your group
  1. Open @BotFather, send /newbot, follow the prompts.
  2. Copy the token it gives you and paste it above.
  3. In your Telegram group: add the bot as a member.
  4. Make it an admin with permission to post — bots cannot post to a group where they are only a member.
  5. Send any message in the group so it appears in the chat picker.

The token is stored in app.db, which is git-ignored — it never reaches the public repo.
